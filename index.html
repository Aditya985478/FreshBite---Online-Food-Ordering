It started with a truck and a dream 🚚

In 2019, founder Aarav Sharma parked a battered orange food truck at a street corner with a simple menu and one belief – good food should travel well.

Word spread. The truck became a kitchen, the kitchen became eight partner restaurants, and today FreshBite delivers over 12,000 orders a month – every single one packed like it was for family.

We still taste every new dish ourselves before it goes on the menu. If we wouldn't order it twice, it doesn't make the cut.
